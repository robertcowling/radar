Matrix cells read impact then likelihood. Impact: 1 Minimal · 2 Minor · 3 Significant · 4 Severe. Likelihood: 1 very low · 2 low · 3 medium · 4 high. So 21 is Minor impact at very low likelihood, 41 is Severe impact at very low likelihood.

Data: Flood Forecasting Centre Flood Guidance Statement, per-county five-day export, archived on each issue (~10:30 UK daily, occasionally twice a day). Columns are aligned by valid date, so each day of the later issue sits directly beneath the same day of the earlier one. Comparisons need at least three overlapping days.
