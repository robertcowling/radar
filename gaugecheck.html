Gauge QC checks

  • THRESHOLD — single 15-min reading ≥ 25 mm (suspicious) or ≥ 50 mm (physically impossible for the UK).
  • SPIKE — isolated peak ≥ 8× the preceding 1¾ hr median, or a sudden jump from zero above 5 mm.
  • SPATIAL — high reading (≥ 8 mm) when all stations within 20 km show near-zero.
  • ZERO — gauge stuck at 0 for 6+ hrs while neighbours and/or radar confirm active rainfall.
  • RADAR — reading far exceeds the Met Office radar QPE at the same location (ratio > 15×).
  • DRIP — near-constant low-amplitude tips over 3+ hrs: classic blocked or leaking tipping bucket signature.
  • ACCUM — 24 hr gauge total ≥ 3.5× the mean of its nearest neighbours.
  • LLM review — AI independently assesses the first flagged run when ≥ 2 checks fail or reading ≥ 20 mm. Verdict: GENUINE, UNCERTAIN, or SUSPECT.
  • 🟠 ELEVATED — 1 check failed.  🔴 FLAGGED — 2 or more checks failed.
