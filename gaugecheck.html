How Gauge QC Works

📡 15-min rainfall reading arrives from telemetry
↓
7 Automated Checks
THRESHOLD Hard limit — single slot ≥ 25 mm is suspicious; ≥ 50 mm is physically impossible for the UK.
SPIKE Temporal spike — isolated peak ≥ 8× the preceding 1h 45min median, or a jump from zero above 5 mm.
SPATIAL Nearest-neighbour — high reading (≥ 8 mm) when all stations within 20 km report near-zero.
ZERO Persistent zero — gauge stuck at 0 for 6 hrs while ≥ 3 neighbours and/or radar show active rainfall.
RADAR Radar conformity — gauge reading far exceeds the Met Office radar QPE at the same location (ratio > 15×).
DRIP Drip / leak pattern — near-constant low-amplitude tips over ≥ 3 hrs (classic blocked or leaking tipping bucket).
ACCUM Accumulation anomaly — 24-hr gauge total is ≥ 3.5× the mean of its nearest neighbours.
