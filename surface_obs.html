Station plot legend

Red = temperature (°C) · Green = dew point (°C)
Circle fill = total cloud cover (oktas) · barb = wind (knots, half=5, full=10, pennant=50)
Symbol left of circle = present weather
Top-right = MSLP, standard synoptic shorthand (last 3 digits of tenths-of-hPa, leading digit dropped) — not a raw hPa reading: "209" means 1020.9 hPa, "987" means 998.7 hPa. Full value is always in the tooltip.

Coverage note

The Met Office ASDI feed doesn't currently publish dew point or sea-level pressure directly (confirmed empty at source, not a QC artefact) — both are derived here instead (Magnus formula from temp + RH for dew point; barometric reduction using station-level pressure and a cached DEM altitude for MSLP) and marked "derived" in each station's tooltip. MSLP is only derivable where a station reports station-level pressure, so its coverage is lower than dew point's.
