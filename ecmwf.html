About this data

  • ECMWF HRES forecast: ECMWF's high-resolution deterministic (IFS) model, full ~9km native resolution, out to 15 days ahead. 00Z/12Z runs cover the full horizon; 06Z/18Z runs only go out to 6 days. CC BY 4.0, via Open-Meteo's Open Data redistribution of ECMWF's own forecast — ECMWF plan to offer this resolution directly later in 2026.
  • Catchment / region / county boundaries: shown for geographic reference only in this view — National River Flow Archive (2014, NERC EIDC) and Office for National Statistics (ONS), Open Government Licence.
  • Compare with: the Met Office UKV forecast for an even higher-resolution (2km), shorter-range (out to ~72h) view of the UK.
