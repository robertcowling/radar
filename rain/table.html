info Data gaps

Each gauge is checked for how much data it actually reported over the selected window. This filter hides stations missing more than the chosen share of readings, so the table isn't cluttered with unreliable or offline gauges. Gaps are usually caused by temporary faults at the Environment Agency, Natural Resources Wales or SEPA APIs — not by an absence of rainfall. Choose "Show all" to see every gauge regardless of missing data.
