info About this Data

This page shows the latest issued Rapid Flood Guidance (Rfg). Rfgs are ad-hoc documents issued by the Flood Forecasting Centre outside the regular daily flood guidance statement cycles.

They are typically triggered by fast-responding flood hazards, such as sudden heavy convective downpours leading to flash flooding risk, where local authorities and emergency responders need immediate tactical guidance.

Operational Support This website is not operationally supported. Refer directly to official Ffc channels for emergency management decision-making.
